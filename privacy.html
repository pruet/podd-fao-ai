Official Policy

Privacy Policy for FAO-PODD

Effective Date: October 6, 2026 • Last Updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the FAO-PODD mobile application and integrated backend services (collectively, the "Application" or "Service"), developed in collaboration with the Participatory One Health Disease Detection (PODD) program, Chiang Mai University (CMU), the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), and the Department of Livestock and Fisheries (DLF) Lao PDR through the Lao Animal Health Information System (LAHIS).

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality and security of any data processed through our application. This policy details how we collect, handle, disclose, and protect personal and surveillance information, in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver AI-assisted animal disease diagnosis, surveillance reporting, and outbreak monitoring, we collect the following types of information:

A. Camera, Photos, and Media

  • Data Collected: Photos captured via device camera or chosen from gallery depicting livestock, animal symptoms, lesions, or carcasses.
  • Purpose: Processed to perform visual AI analysis, determine animal species, identify clinical symptoms, and deliver immediate veterinary response guidelines.
  • Permissions Requested: CAMERA, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (requested at runtime when taking or selecting a photo).

B. Location Data

  • Data Collected: Precise GPS coordinates or approximate location (village name, district, province).
  • Purpose: Crucial for spatial disease surveillance, mapping outbreaks, automated cluster detection for priority reportable diseases, and alerting local livestock officers.
  • Permissions Requested: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (collected during incident submission).

C. Clinical and Symptom Descriptions

  • Data Collected: Species, observed symptoms, animal behavior, mortality/morbidity counts, and reporter notes.
  • Purpose: Analyzed alongside images by veterinary AI models to evaluate against priority reportable animal diseases.

D. Device and Diagnostic Information

  • Data Collected: Device model, OS version, application version, IP address, and API request performance logs.
  • Purpose: Maintaining application security, troubleshooting errors, and monitoring service latency.

E. User & Reporter Credentials (Where Applicable)

  • Data Collected: Volunteer name, phone number, volunteer ID, or authorized LAHIS integration credentials.
  • Purpose: Authorizing livestock reporting volunteers and veterinary officers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used strictly for public animal health and disease surveillance objectives:

  • AI Veterinary Diagnostics: Identifying species and potential reportable diseases using Google Gemini multimodal AI.
  • Outbreak & Cluster Detection: Aggregating village incident timelines to detect prolonged or spreading disease clusters.
  • Surveillance Integration: Transmitting verified findings and officer recommendations to the LAHIS platform.
  • System Reliability: Monitoring API performance, token usage, and system uptime.

Zero Commercial Monetization: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize user data. We do not use user data for targeted advertising or commercial profiling.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We share necessary data with trusted technological and government partners exclusively to fulfill the app's public health function:

  • Google Cloud & Gemini GenAI API: Photos and symptom descriptions are transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 to Google Gemini models to produce structured diagnostic outputs. Enterprise API terms apply; data is not used to train public consumer models without consent.
  • Animal Health Authorities (LAHIS / DLF / FAO): Reports and outbreak coordinates are synchronized with official veterinary departments for response coordination.
  • Google Cloud Platform: Secure cloud infrastructure (App Engine, Datastore) for backend hosting and encrypted database storage.

5. Data Security and Retention

  • Security in Transit & Rest: All communications use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption. Backend data stores utilize access-controlled authentication.
  • Retention Period: Epidemiological surveillance reports are retained as required for official animal health record-keeping and scientific longitudinal analysis. Temporary files and image uploads are purged according to maintenance schedules.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data or submitted reports.

How to Request Data Deletion:

To request deletion of your account or personal information, email pruet.b@eng.cmu.ac.th with the subject Data Deletion Request - FAO-PODD, providing your name, phone number or volunteer ID, and details of the data to be removed.

Requests are verified and processed within 30 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for adult animal health workers, livestock farmers, and veterinarians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). If we discover inadvertent collection of a child's information, we promptly delete it.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational or regulatory improvements. Any revisions will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
