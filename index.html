About the Application

FAO-PODD V.2 is an intelligent veterinary disease surveillance service developed to assist veterinarians, livestock officers, and community volunteers in rapid detection and response to reportable livestock diseases.

The system combines Google Gemini multimodal AI vision with strict disease whitelist guardrails, automatically verifying animal symptoms and synchronizing reports with the Lao Animal Health Information System (LAHIS) and PODD One Health platform.

Key Capabilities

  • Multimodal Image Analysis: Evaluates photos of sick animals or lesions alongside field descriptions to identify species and clinical signs.
  • Strict Whitelist Guardrail: Constrained exclusively to official Priority Reportable Diseases, preventing false or unlisted diagnoses.
  • Trilingual Support: Provides complete diagnostic outputs and veterinary reasoning in Lao (ພາສາລາວ), Thai (ภาษาไทย), and English.
  • Outbreak Cluster Detection: Tracks incident history across villages to detect high-priority reportable disease clusters and alert authorities immediately.
  • Immediate Field Guidelines: Delivers actionable first-response recommendations for local farmers and veterinarians without requiring lab equipment.
  • LAHIS / OHTK Integration: Bi-directional webhook synchronization, dynamic photo fetching, and automated expert review comments.
